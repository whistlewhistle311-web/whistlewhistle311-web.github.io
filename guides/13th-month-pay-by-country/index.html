13th month pay by country (2026)

Many countries require employers to pay an extra month's salary, or part of one, before Christmas. The name, the formula and the deadline change from country to country. This table puts eight of them side by side, each with a free calculator using 2026 figures.

What they have in common

  • It is prorated. If you joined, left or took unpaid leave during the year, you get a share based on the time you worked.
  • Resigning does not cancel it. In all eight countries the proportional amount is part of your final pay.
  • It usually counts only regular pay. Overtime and commissions count in some countries (Ecuador, Brazil averages) and not in others (Philippines basic salary only).

Plan where it goes

A 13th month is the one time of year many people get a lump sum. Deciding before payday what part goes to debt, savings and gifts keeps it from disappearing in December. The Christmas budget planner and the free 2027 budget spreadsheet help with that.
