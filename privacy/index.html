Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy covers K-Beauty Now (whistlewhistle311-web.github.io/kbeauty-now), the TOPIK writing guides (whistlewhistle311-web.github.io/topik-q54) and the K-Beauty Now Pinterest integration.

What we collect

The sites have no accounts, forms, comments or analytics scripts, and set no cookies of their own. The sites are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Pinterest integration

Our Pinterest app is used only to publish pins to our own K-Beauty Now Pinterest account. It does not read, store or share data about other Pinterest users. The access token is stored as an encrypted secret and used only to create pins and boards on our account.

Links to stores

Some product links go to third-party stores or affiliate networks. When you click them, those sites may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies.
