Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy covers paybonuscalc.com (budget, salary and bonus calculators, free spreadsheets and guides), K-Beauty Now (koreanbeautynow.com), the TOPIK writing guides and our Pinterest integration.

Calculators and what you type

Every calculator runs in your browser. The numbers and names you type are not sent to us or stored on a server. Secret Santa and draw links carry the result inside the link itself, so only people who have the link can open it.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not track you across sites. The sites are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Advertising

We may show ads served by Google. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Links to stores

Some links go to stores such as Gumroad, Amazon or Coupang. Some are paid products of ours or affiliate links, and they are labeled. When you follow them, that store's privacy policy applies.

Pinterest integration

Our Pinterest app is used only to publish pins to our own accounts. It does not read, store or share data about other Pinterest users.

Children

The sites are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect personal information.
