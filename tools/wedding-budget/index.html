How to use the split

Start from the big three

Catering, venue and photography usually take the largest shares. Decide those first; the rest adjusts around them.

Cost per guest is the lever

Each extra guest adds to catering, drinks, stationery, favors and often venue size. Cutting the guest list is usually the fastest way to bring the total down.

Keep a contingency

Fees, tips, overtime and last-minute changes show up late. A small contingency share keeps them from coming out of a category you care about.

About the default split

The starting percentages are a planning template, not survey data. Move them to match your priorities and local prices.
